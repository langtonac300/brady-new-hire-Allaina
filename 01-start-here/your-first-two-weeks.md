# Your first two weeks, day by day

Ten days, laid out. After this the plan goes back to weekly themes — the first fortnight is
the one worth scheduling to the day, because it's the stretch where everything is new at
once and it's hard to tell what to do next.

> **This is a plan, not a contract.** Days slip. Meetings move. Access takes longer than it
> should. If a day gets pushed, push it — say so and the rest slides. Nothing here is a
> deadline you can miss.

Two things run alongside every day below:

- **Keep [`cheat-sheet.md`](../02-learning/cheat-sheet.md) open.** Not to read, to look
  things up in. You'll hear four unfamiliar terms an hour for a while.
- **Post your three lines at the end of each day** (what you did, what you learned, what
  surprised you) — see [`how-the-ramp-works.md`](./how-the-ramp-works.md).

The day layout assumes you start on a Monday. If you don't, slide the rows.

---

## Week 1 — getting in, and what this job is

The whole week is orientation. **You are not behind at any point this week**, whatever it
feels like.

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| **1 · Getting in** | Check every login works — Google Ads, the spend dashboard, chat, calendar. **Say something today if one doesn't**; access takes far longer to fix than to request. Meet the team — [`who-everyone-is.md`](./who-everyone-is.md) is the map | Fill in your [Day-1 baseline](../05-self-assessment/baseline.md) — **before any training**, that's the whole point. Read [`how-the-ramp-works.md`](./how-the-ramp-works.md) |
| **2 · What paid search is** | [`ppc-fundamentals.md`](../02-learning/ppc-fundamentals.md), first half | Shadow Alex or Courtney through a normal day. You're watching for the *shape* of it — what gets opened, in what order, what gets ignored. Don't try to take notes on everything |
| **3 · The rest of it** | [`ppc-fundamentals.md`](../02-learning/ppc-fundamentals.md), second half | Start your questions-and-unknowns doc in `04-my-work/`. Sit in on whichever standing team meetings fall today — silent, just listening to how people talk about the numbers |
| **4 · Your first account tour** | Skim the opening section of [`pdc-primer.md`](../02-learning/pdc-primer.md) — just enough to know what the accounts are called. You're not learning it yet | [**T1-1 · Account tour, narrated**](../03-projects/t1-01-account-tour.md), paired with Alex. Your first time inside a real account |
| **5 · Write it up** | Write up T1-1 — the one-page structure map | Settle with Alex **where your work lives**: which board or folder, and how he wants a change posted for review. Cheap to agree now, annoying to fix in week six |

**By Friday you should have:** a filled-in baseline, a long list of questions and a
one-page map of an account you'd never seen on Monday. That's the week.

## Week 2 — how Brady measures things

**This is the important week.** Almost everything else on the reading list you'd eventually
absorb by doing the job. Measurement is the exception: get it wrong and you can work
confidently for a month and be confidently wrong the whole time.

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| **6 · First pass** | [`how-brady-measures.md`](../02-learning/how-brady-measures.md). **Expect not to follow all of it.** Mark the parts that don't land rather than re-reading until they do | [**T1-3 · Keyword vs. search term**](../03-projects/t1-03-keyword-vs-search-term.md), solo. Concrete, after a heavy morning |
| **7 · What counts as a conversion** | Re-read the conversion-tracking section of [`ppc-fundamentals.md`](../02-learning/ppc-fundamentals.md) | [**T1-4 · Conversion-action inventory**](../03-projects/t1-04-conversion-action-inventory.md), paired with Alex |
| **8 · Second pass** | [`how-brady-measures.md`](../02-learning/how-brady-measures.md) again. It reads differently now that you've seen the conversion actions | Start your one-pager: **how a dollar of PDC spend becomes a dollar of reported revenue**, in your own words. Being wrong on paper is the cheapest possible place to be wrong |
| **9 · Sourcing a number** | Finish the one-pager draft | [**T1-5 · Source and caveat the slide**](../03-projects/t1-05-source-and-caveat-the-slide.md), paired with Alex. This is the one that shows whether the week landed |
| **10 · Review** | Fix the one-pager using what T1-5 exposed | Walk it through with Alex. Then read [`pdc-primer.md`](../02-learning/pdc-primer.md) properly — from here on the accounts are the subject |

> ⚠️ **If the attribution material still feels slippery on Day 10, that is the normal
> outcome, not a bad sign.** It's the thing most people need three passes and one real
> mistake to internalize. Say it's still unclear — that sentence is useful information for
> Alex and it costs you nothing.
