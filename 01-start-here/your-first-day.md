## 10:00–10:30 · Getting in

Badge, desk, laptop.

Then, the moment the laptop is in front of you, **try every login**:

- **Google Ads** — you should see the PDC accounts
- **The MTD Spend dashboard** — the team's spend and pacing sheet
- **Google Chat** — where the team actually talks
- **Your calendar** — the standing meetings should already be on it

> ### ⚠️ This is the single most time-critical thing you do today
>
> **If any of them doesn't work, tell Alex before you walk into the huddle.** Not this
> afternoon, not tomorrow.
>
> Access requests take days. A request raised at 10:20 on Tuesday can be fixed by Wednesday;
> the same request raised on Thursday costs you most of week one. **Nobody thinks less of you
> for a broken login — it is IT's queue, not your mistake.** The only version of this that
> costs anything is the quiet one.

## 10:30–12:00 · The L10 huddle

**Normally Tuesday at 10:00 — moved to 10:30 this week only, so you can be there from the
first minute rather than walking in halfway.** From next week it's back to 10:00.

Ninety minutes, seven segments. Read
[`the-l10-huddle.md`](../02-learning/the-l10-huddle.md) beforehand if there's time — but
don't lose sleep over it, you'll read it properly in week two.

**What to do: listen, and nothing else.** Nobody expects you to speak.

Two things worth knowing before you walk in:

- **The first five minutes are personal, on purpose.** The Segue is one good-news item each,
  no work talk. It's the easiest possible way to hear everyone's name attached to a face.
- **The middle hour will be opaque, and that's correct.** Sixty of the ninety minutes are
  IDS — the team solving real problems on accounts you haven't seen yet. **You are not
  supposed to follow it today.** Notice the *shape*: who talks, how a decision gets made,
  what "off track" sounds like out loud.

> **Write down two things you didn't understand.** They're the first two entries in your
> questions doc, and they're better questions than anything you could invent later.

## 13:30–14:15 · Your Day-1 baseline

Go to [`05-self-assessment/baseline.md`](../05-self-assessment/baseline.md) and rate yourself
across the 31 skills.

> ### ⚠️ Do this before any training — that's the whole point
>
> It only works as a **baseline** if it's taken before you learn anything here. At Day 90 you
> re-score the same list, and the gap between the two is the clearest evidence of progress
> you'll get all quarter.
>
> **Rate yourself honestly low.** A page of 1s and 2s on day one is exactly right and it is
> not a judgment on you — it's a map. Inflating it now only steals from the Day-90 comparison,
> and it's you the comparison is for.

## 15:30–16:00 · Close with Alex

Bring:

1. **Anything that didn't work** — logins especially, if it's still outstanding
2. **The two things from the huddle** you didn't understand
3. **Anything that surprised you**

Then post **your three lines** in chat before you log off: what you worked on, one thing you
learned, one thing that surprised you.

**It takes two minutes and nobody is grading it.** The third line is the useful one — if
something surprised you, either the material is wrong or it's incomplete, and both are worth
knowing on day one rather than week six.

## If the laptop isn't ready

It happens, and it's the most likely thing to go wrong today — equipment lead times are the
part of onboarding nobody controls.

**Everything from 13:30 onward works on paper.** The baseline and
[`about-how-you-work.md`](./about-how-you-work.md) are both just questions; print them or
write the answers longhand and type them up later. The huddle, lunch and both conversations
are unaffected.

**Say so early and the day still works.** See
[`when-youre-stuck-or-idle.md`](./when-youre-stuck-or-idle.md).
