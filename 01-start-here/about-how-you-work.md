# About how you work

Fifteen minutes, in your first couple of days. Bring it to your first conversation with Alex.

> **This is not a test and it isn't stored anywhere formal.** The whole ramp was designed
> before anyone had met you, which means it's built on guesses. This is how we replace the
> guesses with facts and change the plan where it's wrong.
>
> **Honest answers make the next twelve weeks better. Flattering ones make them worse** — if
> you say you're comfortable with something you're not, we'll skip past it and you'll be
> quietly stuck in week six.
>
> It pairs with [`../05-self-assessment/baseline.md`](../05-self-assessment/baseline.md),
> which covers *what you can do*. This one is about *how you like to work*.
