# Self-assessment — your Day-1 baseline

Fill this in during your **first few days, before any training.** Then fill in the same
sheet again at **Day 90** and put them side by side.

**This is yours.** It isn't a test, it isn't scored by anyone else, and it doesn't go into
any review. Its only job is to show you how far you moved.

---

## ⚠️ Read this before you rate yourself — it will save you a bad week

On the things you haven't learned yet, your Day-1 rating will probably be **too high**.
That isn't overconfidence — it's that you can't see the shape of something you haven't
learned. It happens to everyone.

**So some of your scores may go *down* at Day 30 before they go up.** If that happens, it
means the training is working and you've discovered what you didn't know. It is not a sign
you're doing badly. Expect it, and don't let it rattle you.

The one where this hits hardest is **measurement and attribution**. It looks simple until
you've been burned by it once.

---

## How to score

- 🔴 **Not yet** — I couldn't do this or explain it
- 🟡 **Developing** — I could have a go, with help or with checking
- 🟢 **Independent** — I could do this on my own and be confident I was right
