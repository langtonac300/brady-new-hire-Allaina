## The point

You can't work on an account you can't describe. This is the first project because
everything after it assumes you can open PDC Healthcare and say what's in there — and
because saying it out loud, to someone who knows the account, is how you find out which
bits you only *think* you understand.

It's also the template. T1-2 does the same tour on a very different account, and the
comparison is where the actual lesson lands.

---

## What to do

0. **Before you open anything, write down what you expect to find.** Three lines. How many
   campaigns, what types, whether brand is a big or small share. **"I have no idea" is a
   fine answer** — it makes the gap visible afterwards, which is the point. See
   [`how-to-run-a-project.md`](../02-learning/how-to-run-a-project.md).
1. **Open PDC Healthcare.** It's in the **Brady Global MCC** — not the one called
   "Brady US - MCC (Seton/Emed)", which doesn't contain Brady US either. That naming trap
   is in the [cheat sheet](../02-learning/cheat-sheet.md); it catches everyone once.
2. **List the campaigns that are actually spending.** Filter to the last 30 days and sort by
   cost. Ignore the paused archaeology for now.

   **Cap it at the top ten by cost.** If there are fewer, do all of them. If there are
   forty, the top ten will be most of the spend and the rest is a long tail you can come
   back to — trying to do all forty is how this becomes a three-hour project.
3. **For each one, write down four things:**

   | Column | What goes in it |
   |--------|-----------------|
   | Campaign type | Search / PMax / DemandGen / Shopping / DSA |
   | Bidding strategy | tROAS, tCPA, Maximize conversions, etc. — and the target if there is one |
   | **What it's optimizing to** | Which conversion action(s) the strategy is chasing |
   | Brand or non-brand | Which side of the split it sits on |

4. **Mark which are brand and which are non-brand,** and note how you could tell.
5. **Finish with two sentences:** what is this account trying to do, and how would you know
   if it stopped doing it?

---

## ⚠️ The column people skip

The third one — *what it's optimizing to*. PDC is **already fully on smart bidding**, so
you don't set bids here; the conversion goal is the steering wheel. An account can be
perfectly well structured and still be aimed at the wrong thing.

Note what you find and move on. **T1-4 goes after it properly** — this project is just
making sure you noticed there's a column there.

---

## What good looks like

- Someone who has never opened the account could read your map and know what's in it.
- Every row has all four columns filled — including the third one.
- You asked at least two questions during the hour. This one is paired precisely so that
  the things you're unsure about get corrected now, before they set.

**If it runs long:** stop at an hour and say where you got to. A tour that took three hours
was scoped wrong, and that's useful feedback rather than a failure.
