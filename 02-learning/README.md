## If you read only one thing carefully

Make it **`how-brady-measures.md`**.

Almost everything else on this list you'd eventually pick up by doing the job. Measurement
is the exception: if you get it wrong, you can work confidently for weeks and be
confidently wrong the whole time — quoting numbers in meetings that don't mean what you
think they mean. It's also the thing most likely to feel unclear the first three times you
read it, which is normal and not a reflection on you.
